About EmotiSense 💙

EmotiSense is a free web-based assistive learning platform designed to support the socio-emotional learning of Filipino children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD). It provides a safe, interactive, and beginner-friendly environment for learners aged 6–12.

🎯

What We Offer

Emotion recognition mini-games, guided social situation simulations, and calming activities using culturally appropriate Filipino-based content.

💛

Our Purpose

Supporting learners, parents, caregivers, and SPED teachers through accessible socio-emotional learning tools — as an educational support system.

🤝

Who We Help

Designed for parents, caregivers, SPED teachers, therapists, and psychiatrists to monitor and support learners with ASD aged 6–12.

Interactive Activities 🎮

Three thoughtfully designed modules that guide learners through emotions, social skills, and calming strategies.

🎭

Emotion Games

Interactive mini-games that help learners recognize and understand the six basic emotions: happiness, sadness, anger, fear, disgust, and surprise. Activities include animated flashcards, facial expression matching, and real-life situation analysis.

🤝

Social Simulations

Guided scenarios that teach appropriate social responses and decision-making skills. Learners practice empathy and kindness through real-life Filipino-based situations such as helping an elderly person cross the street or supporting a classmate in need.

🌊

Calming Activities

Simple activities that support relaxation and emotional self-regulation. Includes guided breathing exercises with soothing ocean wave sounds and interactive fidget-inspired activities to help learners stay calm and focused.
